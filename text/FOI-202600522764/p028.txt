5. Spatial Mapping


The high level mapping produced to date was discussed with the group, with the need to
develop a single source of material that identiﬁes opportunities and synergies between
potential development sites and supporting/enabling infrastructure and initiatives.
Information on constraining/limiting factors could also be overlayed, providing a system
wide view of investment opportunities.



Action: Policy areas are to consider what data sets they hold that could be used to
augment data centre mapping. For example, this could extend to; District Heat
Networks; Existing/planned Hydrogen Generation, Tech and entrepreneur
ecosystems, NHS estate or other heat demand sites. Follow up email to be shared
with the group after separate meeting on May 26th to seek further thoughts.

Owners: All working group members to respond with potential data sets from their policy
area which are relevant.



Community Benefits


Persistent negative public perception was noted, with a need to identify the mechanisms
that would best identify community sentiment and potential opportunities for tailored
beneﬁts.



It was noted that there will be a large amount of evidence in
England/Ireland/Netherlands/Nordics from existing developments. An international
comparison could identify routes to drive community engagement.



Given this is a highly capitalised industry, there is potential to leverage signiﬁcant
contributions if handled correctly. Similar examples such as approaches taken with the
housing industry could be explored.
