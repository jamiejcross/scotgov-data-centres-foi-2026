Background



      National Planning Framework 4 (NPF4) designates ‘green data centres’ as
       national developments, establishing the principle of development while leaving
       detailed design and environmental assessments for further consideration.
      All planning applications are considered on their individual merits
      To be considered a 'green data centre', planning authorities may wish to consider
       criteria such as the extent to which a data centre is:
           o powered from renewable energy sources;
           o makes use of energy e icient technologies;
           o seeks to minimise water consumption; and
           o supports the re-use of excess heat.
