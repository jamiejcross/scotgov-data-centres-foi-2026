As discussed, Apatura has undertaken a signiﬁcant amount of analysis on the
economic impact that data centre investment could have in Scotland, both at a national
level and at a more local, site-speciﬁc level. This work has been compiled by Glic and
validated by Scottish Enterprise. Attached are a small number of example reports
(including Ravenscraig for illustration), but equivalent economic impact assessments
have been prepared across all of our sites. We would be very happy to talk through this
work in more detail, support any further analysis that might be helpful, or collectively
think about what additional evidence or framing could be most useful from an
economic development perspective.



We also wanted to share the Scottish Green Data Centre Charter, which Apatura has
developed and plans to launch in mid-May. This is a short, fact-based, industry-level
document that responds directly to a question we hear frequently from planners, local
authorities and communities: “What is a green data centre in practice?” The Charter
sets out clear, referenced principles on electricity use, water use, energy e iciency and
heat, with the aim of providing greater certainty and consistency in how proposals are
understood and assessed.



Importantly, this is not an Apatura-branded report. Our intention is to launch the
Charter as a pan-industry initiative, supported by a small group of inﬂuential industry
co-signatories. We would very much welcome Scottish Government involvement
through a short supportive quote. We are moving quickly here, and it would be very
helpful if you could indicate your willingness to support no later than midday, Friday May
15.
