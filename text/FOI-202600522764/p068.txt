Dear [Redacted - S.38(1)(b)] - I hope you are well.

Further to our recent discussions, and ongoing engagement with the team, we
wanted to share a brief update on two developments at Apatura.

Firstly, we have now submitted a planning application to Falkirk Council for our
proposed Larbert Data Centre Campus, which you may recall we discussed
previously. This follows a number of important recent milestones, including
confirmation that the project has been accepted onto the Connection Acceleration
Service (CAS), which is a significant step forward in progressing delivery.

Secondly, we have now launched the industry charter Delivering green data centres
in Scotland which we shared in draft previously. The Charter is a short, industry-
facing document that seeks to bring greater clarity and consistency to what
constitutes a “green” data centre in practice. It sets out a series of clear and
referenced principles across electricity use, water use, energy efficiency and heat,
including a requirement for all new developments to be district heat ready – enabling
the potential for waste heat to be repurposed to provide affordable, low carbon heat
to homes, businesses and communities.

Our intention is that this is not seen as an Apatura document, but as a framework for
the wider industry. It has been supported by a number of organisations including
Prosper, Heat Network Scotland, the Renewable Energy Association and
Ravenscraig Ltd, and we hope it can play a useful role in supporting both policy
development and planning decision-making.

When we met in April you thought it might be an idea to have a meeting the new
Cabinet Secretary when appropriate - something that we would welcome.
