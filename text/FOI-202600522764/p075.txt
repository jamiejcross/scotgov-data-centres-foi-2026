Water Considerations



There has been a material shift in how the data centre industry thinks about water over
the last ~2–5 years where it is looking to: reducing potable water use, replenishing local
watersheds (where needed) and using non-potable or recycled water. This has been
coupled with improved technologies such as hybrid cooling systems (utilising air-
cooled chillers in places with temperate climates like Scotland) and liquid
cooling (utilising closed loop systems) to re-use water.

It is important to remember that, from a Scottish perspective, there is a disconnect
between the reality of how much water is needed (and how innovative we need to be) vs
the public perception.
