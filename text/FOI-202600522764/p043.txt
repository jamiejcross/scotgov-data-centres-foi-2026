the event last week.

As discussed, Apatura has undertaken a significant amount of analysis on the
economic impact that data centre investment could have in Scotland, both at a
national level and at a more local, site-specific level. This work has been compiled by
Glic and validated by Scottish Enterprise. Attached are a small number of example
reports (including Ravenscraig for illustration), but equivalent economic impact
assessments have been prepared across all of our sites. We would be very happy to
talk through this work in more detail, support any further analysis that might be
helpful, or collectively think about what additional evidence or framing could be most
useful from an economic development perspective.

We also wanted to share the Scottish Green Data Centre Charter, which Apatura
has developed and plans to launch in mid-May. This is a short, fact-based,
industry-level document that responds directly to a question we hear frequently from
planners, local authorities and communities: “What is a green data centre in
practice?” The Charter sets out clear, referenced principles on electricity use, water
use, energy efficiency and heat, with the aim of providing greater certainty and
consistency in how proposals are understood and assessed.
