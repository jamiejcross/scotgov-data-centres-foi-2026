     The table is useful but should capture developers, operators and investors, and
      its current RAG criteria need strengthening to include grid capacity and queue
      position, site scale, and environmental and water considerations, alongside a
      clearer, communicable deﬁnition of a “green” data centre.
     Uncertainty around planning data sources was noted, including access to local
      authority systems and integration with wider datasets.
     Continued stakeholder engagement through the CWG remains essential to
      broaden external input and secure sustained commitment.



2.2 Emerging Requirements

     Emerging requirements focus on developing a live, data-driven status view,
      drawing on planning and Improvement Service datasets, with a clear and explicit
      purpose around delivery proximity, scale, prioritisation and value leverage.
     [Redacted - S.29]
     The system must become dynamic and data-led, supported by a clear purpose
      statement and complemented by visibility above the triage through an SG-level
      strategic risk register.
