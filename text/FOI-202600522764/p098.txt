Yes, we are engaged with water policy colleagues as well as Scottish Water. The current
draft of forthcoming AI Strategy infrastructure chapter has this excerpt below relating to
water;



“Water infrastructure



As AI adoption accelerates, ensuring that supporting infrastructure is developed in a
water-responsible manner in Scotland is of paramount importance. The growing
presence of data centres brings increased demand for water resources, making
sustainable management of this precious asset a critical consideration. Scottish Water
is therefore advising the data centre industry on several fronts: guiding location
decisions to support the most sustainable use of water; encouraging the reuse of
treated wastewater; and promoting the adoption of closed-loop systems to reduce
overall demand”.



The piece around induced and direct beneﬁts probably meris a discussion. There is a
very limited evidence base around AI infrastructure globally given it is a new
technology. It leans more towards attracting digital tech (and broader) investment
levels to sit in proximity to the data centres. Most local authorities we are dealing with
see these as catalytic investments and we are working with enterprise agencies to
support.
