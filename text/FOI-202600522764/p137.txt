          HOT ISSUE: Data Centres

          ISSUE

          Scotland’s abundant renewable energy resources position it as a leader in green data hosting.
          timely and proportionate planning and consenting are key enablers of Scotland’s digital future.
          Stakeholder concerns about the impact of these developments are also being reported in the m
          raised directly with o icials, and there have been calls to deﬁne ‘green data centres’.

      National Planning Framework 4 (NPF4) designates ‘green data centres’ as
       national developments, establishing the principle of development and giving
       certainty for investors and communities. Policy also supports the roll out of
       digital infrastructure to unlock economic potential across Scotland.
      All applications are subject to site speciﬁc assessments, including
       Environmental Impact Assessments where relevant.
      We have engaged with the sector and local authorities on forthcoming data
       centre proposals, while maintaining separation from decision-making.
      We have also provided surge support and upskilling planning authorities through
       the National Planning Hub to ensure capacity for complex applications, to grow
       Planner’s knowledge and practice of this topic – delivered with key stakeholders
       and industry.
      We have not speciﬁcally deﬁned ‘green data centres’ but we have set out a range
       of criteria to guide consideration of this by planning authorities.
