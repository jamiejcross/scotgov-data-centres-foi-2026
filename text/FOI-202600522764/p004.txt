2. Debrief of Ministerial Meeting with DataVita and CoreWeave


      Director of Economic Development noted Data Centre opportunity has attracted
       attention at the highest levels, including ministers and the First Minister, due to
       its scale and strategic importance.
      Key priority for investors is speed of deployment to meet their expectations.
       Scotland beneﬁts from a well-established planning system, which is supportive
       of data centres under National Planning Framework 4 (NP4).
      There is theoretical competition between the North Lanarkshire site and an AI
       Growth Zone in North East England, linked to UK Government plans for data
       centres and AIGZs.
      The project is viewed as a test case for Scotland’s ability to mobilise quickly with
       strong ministerial backing.
      Director of Economic Development tasked with pulling together a coherent Team
       Scotland response to ensure Scotland secures the investment and does not lose
       out due to public sector inaction.
      Key question: How can the public sector support the investment proposals
       without pre-judging the planning process, while providing assurance that, if
       requirements are met, timelines can be achieved?


3. UKG engagement and potential announcement


      Ongoing engagement with UKG, focused on AI Growth Zones (AIGZ) initiative
      A 500MW data centre is required to be operational and powered by 2030.
      Work is ongoing in close collaboration with Energy Policy and PARD to address
       infrastructure needs.
      NESO connection reform will play a signiﬁcant role in identifying a suitable
       Scottish AIGZ location, but it will not resolve the challenge on its own.
      CoreWeave’s £1.5bn investment into DataVita was initially announced through
       the UKG’s Tech Prosperity Deal.
