Thank you for your participation in the workshop “Building a Sustainable Tomorrow: The
Role of Energy and Data Infrastructure in Scotland” on 21 May. The level of engagement
across policymakers, industry leaders, and academic researchers was extremely
encouraging. It was clear there is a realistic ambition to position Scotland as one of the
key locations globally for Data Centre development. Realising this vision will require
coordinated action and a step change in how we connect research, policy, and industry
decision-making.



In response to this need, the University has initiated the development of a more
structured and sustained engagement initiative, provisionally titled Green Data Centre
Research Hub. The purpose of this email is to solicit your feedback and support for this
proposal.

The Green Data Centre Research Hub would create a co-ordinated national platform
for Scotland that will bring together industry, government, regulators, and academic
expertise to accelerate development of the industry in Scotland by addressing key
policy and technical challenges faced by industry and Governments. We expect the
challenges will span energy systems, economic development, environmental impact,
community beneﬁts, and scaling innovation in the Data Centre sector.
