Strategic Context
Scotland has a strong opportunity to lead in sustainable compute, driven by rising AI
demand and signiﬁcant curtailed renewable energy. However, the CWG emphasised
that Scotland must ﬁrst achieve clear internal alignment on what it can realistically
deliver. From an inward-investment perspective, Scotland needs a credible,
evidence-based proposition, setting out capability, capacity, strengths and constraints,
to avoid engaging investors prematurely and risking market conﬁdence.



A new Director General-level Strategic Small Working Group has been established to set
high-level to consider the strategic approach.




Key Headlines
   Economic value must be central to all GDC activity; need for a coherent, cross-
    agency articulation of “what Scotland wants” from data centre investment.
   Power constraints remain the critical bottleneck, with NESO queue reform exposing
    structural challenges and long timelines (many projects unable to connect until
    ~2035).
   Signiﬁcant opportunity exists to align curtailed wind, micro-grid solutions, and data
    centre demand.
   Lanarkshire identiﬁed as Scotland’s AIGZ by UKG.Initial indication had been for 5
    AIGZs across the UK, but now possibility of up to 10 – brings wider challenges to
    consider including supply chain
   Waste heat reuse, planning readiness and supply chain opportunities remain key
    di erentiators.
   Agencies highlight the need for a clear proposition / pitch deck before further
    investor engagement.
   Strong interest from operators (e.g., CoreWeave) continues without active
    promotion.
